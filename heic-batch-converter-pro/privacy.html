HEIC Batch Converter Pro — Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-12

This policy covers HEIC Batch Converter Pro for Windows, distributed through the Microsoft Store. The app converts the photos you choose (HEIC / HEIF) into JPG or PNG files entirely on your own computer.

  1. No data collection. The app does not collect, store or transmit any personal information, photos, photo metadata (such as location), usage data, telemetry or crash reports to the developer or to any third party.
  2. No network access. The app does not connect to the internet. Licence and trial status are checked by the Microsoft Store client on Windows (Microsoft’s privacy statement applies); the app itself sends nothing. Updates are delivered only through the Microsoft Store.
  3. Your photos stay on your device. The app only reads the photos you add; it never changes, moves or deletes them. Converted files are written only to the folder shown in the app (by default a new folder next to the originals, or a folder you choose). If a photo is stored in a cloud folder such as OneDrive and is not downloaded yet, Windows or the cloud client may download it when the app reads it; the app itself does not.
  4. Photo metadata is your choice. You decide whether the converted files keep all shooting information, have the location removed, or have all of it removed. This happens only in the files written on your computer.
  5. Local application data. Settings, diagnostic logs and the trial usage count are stored only on your computer, under %LOCALAPPDATA%\HeicBatchConverter\ (and, for the installed app, in its local app settings). Logs contain file names, times, conversion options and counts — never photo content or metadata values. The trial usage count contains only a date and two numbers. You can delete this folder at any time.
  6. Your control and access. Because nothing is collected, the developer holds no information about you to access, correct or delete. Everything the app produces is ordinary files and folders on your own disk, under your control.
  7. Children. The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.
  8. Legal compliance. The app is designed to comply with applicable privacy laws (including GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPL, Australia’s Privacy Act 1988 and New Zealand’s Privacy Act 2020) by not collecting personal information in the first place.
  9. Contact. topfreeware@hotmail.com
  10. Changes. Updates to this policy will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
