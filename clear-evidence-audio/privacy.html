Clear Evidence Audio — Privacy Policy (Standard and Free editions)

Effective: 2026-10-06 (previous version: 2026-10-01)

This policy covers Clear Evidence Audio (Standard) and Clear Evidence Audio Free for Windows. Each processes the audio files you choose to open entirely on your own computer. Clear Evidence Audio Pro has its own policy: Privacy Policy (Pro).

  1. No data collection. The app does not collect, store or transmit any personal information, usage data, telemetry, crash reports or audio content to the developer or to any third party.
  2. No network access. The app does not connect to the internet. Licence status is checked by the Microsoft Store client on Windows (Microsoft’s privacy statement applies); the app itself sends nothing. Updates are delivered only through the Microsoft Store.
  3. Your files stay on your device. Recordings you import are copied into the case folder you choose on your own disk. Analysis results, enhanced audio, logs and exported processing packages are written only to locations you select. You can delete them at any time with normal file tools.
  4. Local application data. Settings and diagnostic logs are stored under %LOCALAPPDATA%\ClearEvidenceAudio\ on your computer only. Logs contain file names, timestamps and processing parameters; they never leave your device.
  5. Your control and access. Because nothing is collected, there is nothing held by the developer to access, correct or delete. All data the app produces is in ordinary files and folders on your own disk, under your control.
  6. Children. The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.
  7. Legal compliance. The app is designed to comply with applicable privacy laws (including GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPL, Australia’s Privacy Act 1988 and New Zealand’s Privacy Act 2020) by not collecting personal information in the first place.
  8. Contact. topfreeware@hotmail.com
  9. Changes. Updates to this policy will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
