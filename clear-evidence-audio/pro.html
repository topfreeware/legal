Who each edition is for

Standard — anyone who needs to hear a recording clearly while keeping the original untouched: students and researchers (interviews, oral history, field recordings), journalists and podcasters cleaning up interviews, people keeping recordings about a personal dispute before they talk to a lawyer, small-business owners, HR and property managers, teachers and parents, families organising voice memos.

Pro — people who handle recordings as part of a professional procedure and need the whole workflow traceable: law firms and in-house counsel, arbitration and mediation bodies, notary offices, insurance claim investigators, corporate compliance and internal investigations, regulatory and enforcement units, investigative newsrooms, and research archives processing large collections.

Pro will carry the same fixed statements as Standard: "All processing only attenuates or filters out noise; nothing that was not present in the original recording is added." "This software makes no determination regarding the authenticity, integrity or legal effect of the original recording." Together with the liability statement in the License Terms.
