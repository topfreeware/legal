Who each edition is for

Standard — anyone who needs to hear a recording clearly while keeping the original untouched: students and researchers (interviews, oral history, field recordings), journalists and podcasters cleaning up interviews, people keeping recordings about a personal dispute before they talk to a lawyer, small-business owners, HR and property managers, teachers and parents, families organising voice memos.

Pro — people who handle recordings as part of a professional procedure and need the whole workflow traceable: law firms and in-house counsel, arbitration and mediation bodies, notary offices, insurance claim investigators, corporate compliance and internal investigations, regulatory and enforcement units, investigative newsrooms, and research archives processing large collections.

Both editions carry the same fixed statements: "All processing only attenuates or filters out noise; nothing that was not present in the original recording is added." "This software makes no determination regarding the authenticity, integrity or legal effect of the original recording." Automatic transcription in Pro is generated by an on-device model for locating and searching only and may contain errors; the recording itself remains the record. See the License Terms.

两个版本各适合谁

标准版 —— 需要听清一段录音、同时保证原件不被改动的任何人：学生与研究者（访谈、口述史、田野录音）、记者与播客作者整理采访、在找律师之前先保全纠纷录音的个人、小企业主、HR 与物业、教师与家长、整理家庭语音备忘的用户。

专业版 —— 把录音作为工作流程一部分、需要全程可追溯的专业人员：律师事务所与企业法务、仲裁与调解机构、公证处、保险理赔调查、企业合规与内部调查、监管执法部门、调查报道团队、处理大量录音的研究与档案机构。

两个版本沿用相同的固定声明："所有处理只是衰减或滤除噪声，不会向录音添加任何原本不存在的内容。""本软件不对原始录音的真实性、完整性或法律效力作出任何判断。"专业版的自动转写由本机模型生成，仅供定位与检索，可能有错漏；以录音为准。见使用条款。
