Clear Evidence Audio Pro — Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-06

This policy covers Clear Evidence Audio Pro for Windows. It processes the audio files you choose to open entirely on your own computer, including on-device transcription. The Standard and Free editions have their own policy: Privacy Policy (Standard / Free).

  1. No data collection. The app does not collect, store or transmit any personal information, usage data, telemetry, crash reports, audio content or transcripts to the developer or to any third party.
  2. Network access — transcription models only. The app connects to the internet only when you click Download for a transcription model (in Tools → Model manager or in the Transcribe dialog). It then only requests that model file from GitHub (github.com and GitHub’s file servers); the downloaded file is verified by SHA-256 before use. The app uploads no files and sends no user data — no audio, transcripts, case data, identifiers or usage information. As with any web download, GitHub receives your IP address and standard request information, handled under GitHub’s privacy statement. You can also copy the model files into the model folder yourself; in that case the app does not connect to the internet at all. Licence status is checked by the Microsoft Store client on Windows (Microsoft’s privacy statement applies); the app itself sends nothing. Updates are delivered only through the Microsoft Store.
  3. Your files stay on your device. Recordings you import are copied into the case folder you choose on your own disk. Analysis results, enhanced audio, logs and exported processing packages are written only to locations you select. You can delete them at any time with normal file tools.
  4. Local application data. Settings, diagnostic logs and downloaded transcription models are stored under %LOCALAPPDATA%\ClearEvidenceAudio\ on your computer only. Logs contain file names, timestamps and processing parameters; they never leave your device. Transcripts are generated on your computer and saved only in your case folder.
  5. Your control and access. Because nothing is collected, there is nothing held by the developer to access, correct or delete. All data the app produces is in ordinary files and folders on your own disk, under your control.
  6. Children. The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.
  7. Legal compliance. The app is designed to comply with applicable privacy laws (including GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPL, Australia’s Privacy Act 1988 and New Zealand’s Privacy Act 2020) by not collecting personal information in the first place.
  8. Contact. topfreeware@hotmail.com
  9. Changes. Updates to this policy will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

  7. 法律合规。 本软件通过从一开始就不收集个人信息的方式，满足适用的隐私法律（包括 GDPR、CCPA/CPRA、《个人信息保护法》、澳大利亚 Privacy Act 1988、新西兰 Privacy Act 2020）。
